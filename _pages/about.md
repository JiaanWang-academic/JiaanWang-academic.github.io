Hello! I'm Jiaan Wang (王佳安), a Research Scientist at Weixin AI, Tencent, based in Beijing. I received my B.Eng. and M.S. degrees from Soochow University (2016–2023), where I was advised by [Prof. Zhixu Li](https://sites.google.com/site/zhixuli) (now at Renmin University of China) and [Dr. Fandong Meng](http://fandongmeng.github.io/) (Weixin AI). I have also worked closely with [Dr. Jiarong Xu](https://galina0217.github.io/) (Fudan University) and [Dr. Jianfeng Qu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=aTUbmccAAAAJ) (now at Renmin University of China).

My current research centers on the post-training of large foundation models, with a particular focus on LLM agents and LLM evaluation.

Previously, I worked on a broad range of NLP topics, including:
- **Machine Translation**: [DeepTrans (TACL 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10187), [DRT (ACL 2025 Findings)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17498), [RAGtrans (EMNLP 2025 Findings)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04342) and [ExTrans (arXiv preprint 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12996).
- **Multi-lingual Summarization**: [M2MS empirical study (ACL 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12983), [PISCES (ACL 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09220), [Translationese in CLS (EMNLP 2023 Findings)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07220), [CLS Survey (TACL 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.12515), [ClidSum (EMNLP 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05599).
- **Dialogue Generation**: [Role-Play Tutorial (IJCAI 2025)](https://ijcai-roleplay.github.io/), [EnCo (AAAI 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04361).
- **NLG Evaluation**: [Is ChatGPT a Good NLG Evaluator? (NewSumm@EMNLP 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04048).

Beyond computer science, I also used to have a research interest in **management science**, where I explored how AI techniques can be applied to business and societal problems. This line of work resulted in two papers in **UTD-24** journals: [UPSTAR (ISR)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/isre.2024.1028) on recommender systems and [PPGP (JOC)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/ijoc.2023.0115) on privacy-preserving graph learning.
